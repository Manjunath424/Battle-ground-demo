Phase 2: skydive onto a larger island, loot up, stay inside the shrinking blue zone and outlast the bots.

Mouse / touch: press and drag on the LEFT of the screen = joystick; drag on the RIGHT = aim; on-screen FIRE / JUMP / RELOAD / SWAP buttons.
Keyboard: WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · arrow keys aim · F fire · R reload · 1/2/3 weapons.
Pointer-lock (if your browser allows it): click the game, then mouse aims and left-click fires.
Walk over loot to pick it up: orange rifle · purple SMG · red shotgun · cyan sniper · yellow ammo · green medkit · blue armor.
Skydive: steer while falling, SPACE/JUMP opens the parachute (auto-opens low). Weapons: keys 1-5.
